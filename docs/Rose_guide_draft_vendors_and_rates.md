# Draft for the Rose guide: picking a vendor (half page)

*Draft only. The Rose guide itself has not been edited.*

## Picking a vendor for a booking
1. Open the booking, then **Vendor Options**. The **Vendors and rates** box is at the top.
2. Read the red or grey line **Vendor rates missing**. A missing rate costs $0, so the vendor cost is too low until it is fixed.
3. For each category the booking needs (boat snorkel, boat dive, gear, kayaks, Pennekamp) choose a vendor from the list. Each choice shows the price, the status, the vendor's notes (check-in times, what is included) and how many passengers the boat takes. Choosing a vendor **replaces** the old one in that category; you never end up with two vendors in one category.
4. Fill in **# Snorkelers** and **# Divers** if the group is split. Leave a box blank if you do not know; the app then uses students plus chaperones for snorkelers.
5. Check the cost preview (vendor cost and total package price, before and after). In **Sandbox** nothing is saved. To save, switch to **Live**, press **Review change**, read the box, type the reason and press **Save live change**. The app then re-reads Airtable and shows Airtable's own total next to its estimate. Airtable's figure is the right one.

## What the tags mean
- **Yellow "Pending vendor confirmation"**: the vendor has not confirmed this rate yet. On a real booking you must tick a box to say you know. Ask the vendor to confirm before the school signs.
- **Amber "rate sheet dated ..., after this arrival"**: the only rate we have is dated after the school's arrival date (for example a 2027 sheet for a 2026 trip). You can still choose it, but check the price with the vendor.
- **Grey "confirm before booking"**: call the vendor to confirm availability for the date.

## What a capacity warning means
An amber capacity line means the number of people is over what the vendor's boat takes, or the app has no capacity on file ("capacity not on file"). It never stops you from saving. **Call the vendor** and agree the split. For a mixed group (snorkelers and divers on one boat) the real limit may be lower than either number, and with more than one trip the capacity is per trip, so confirm how the group is split.

## When the app asks for an extra tick
Approved proposals (saving takes the proposal out of Approved, raises the version and alerts Martha; the school's page stays up until it is approved and published again), completed or legacy bookings, and bookings whose contract is Sent or Signed. Stop and ask Martha if you are not sure.
